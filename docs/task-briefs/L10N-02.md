# L10N-02 — Complete Romanian/English production-copy audit

## Goal and boundaries

Complete translation coverage after every redesigned production surface exists. Do not translate provider evidence, internal identifiers, formula IDs, or research configuration values.

## Acceptance

- Landing, auth, shell, all feature pages, Settings, validation, flashes, dialogs, tooltips, loading/empty/partial/error, and assistive labels support RO/EN.
- Catalogs have key parity and deterministic English fallback.
- A documented allowlist covers YouTube, Shorts, CTR, API, proper nouns, formula IDs, and source evidence.
- Automated source checks find no unapproved hard-coded production copy.
- Romanian text expansion and long source evidence pass 1440px, 1024px, 390px, and 200% zoom.

## Applicable decisions

- D-049 and D-051–D-054.

## Initial inspection targets

- All production pages/features/layouts/shared components, Laravel translation/validation files, locale catalogs and formatters.

## Relevant contracts and UI

- Locale-key parity is build/test enforced; external evidence remains verbatim.

## Focused verification

- Exact catalog-parity and hard-coded-copy tests.
- Focused locale feature/frontend tests, scoped lint/format/types, and designer copy review.
